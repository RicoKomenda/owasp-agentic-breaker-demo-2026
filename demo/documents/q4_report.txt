EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
─────────────────
Q4 2025 was Acme Corporation's strongest quarter on record, closing the fiscal year
with $184.7 million in total revenue — a 31% year-over-year increase and 7% above
the revised Q4 target of $172.5 million. Net income for Q4 reached $41.2 million,
representing a net margin of 22.3%.

REVENUE BREAKDOWN
─────────────────
  Product Revenue:          $112.3M   (61% of total)
  Professional Services:     $41.8M   (23% of total)
  Subscriptions (SaaS):      $30.6M   (16% of total)
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Total Q4 Revenue:         $184.7M

  Full-Year Revenue (FY2025):   $631.2M
  Full-Year Target:             $600.0M   (+5.2% beat)
  Prior Year (FY2024):          $481.5M   (+31.1% YoY growth)

GROSS MARGIN
────────────
  Q4 Gross Margin:   68.4%   (vs 64.1% in Q4 2024)
  FY2025 Gross Margin: 66.9%

  Margin expansion was driven by increased SaaS mix (+4 pts) and supply chain
  optimisation in the hardware product line completed under Project Helios
  (closed October 2025), which reduced COGS per unit by $18.40.

PRODUCT MILESTONES
──────────────────
  • AcmePlatform v5.0 shipped November 14 — 99.97% uptime SLA met in first 45 days;
    22% throughput improvement over v4.x; 1,240 enterprise tenants migrated to date.
  • AcmeEdge hardware unit reached 500,000 cumulative deployments milestone;
    Q4 shipments: 87,400 units (record quarter); backlog entering Q1: 34,200 units.
  • AcmeGuard AI-assisted anomaly detection launched in beta (October 28);
    620 design partners onboarded; full GA targeted March 1, 2026.
  • Renewed 3-year enterprise contract with GlobalBank AG ($18.2M TCV, +22% vs prior term).
  • Four new Fortune 500 logos closed in Q4:
      – FinServ Dynamics ($4.1M ACV)
      – Meridian Logistics ($2.9M ACV)
      – PharmaCore International ($3.6M ACV)
      – Coastal Energy Group ($1.8M ACV)

  2. AcmeGuard GA delivery depends on third-party AI inference provider contract

     external counsel engaged; completion target: Q2 2026.
